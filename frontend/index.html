Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common developer, freelancer, and billing questions about the platform.

Is there really a free forever plan?
Yes! Our Starter Plan is 100% free forever and lets you manage up to 5 clients and 5 projects. No credit card is required to sign up.
Can I download or print PDF invoices?
Yes, you can generate invoices directly, assign custom tax rules, and click download to fetch high-quality billing PDFs to share with clients.
How does the data backup and security work?
CreateFlowX utilizes robust, industry-standard SQL databases and token authentication. Your credentials and client ledgers are encrypted and securely locked.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan at any time?
Absolutely. You can choose to upgrade to our Pro plan from your Settings menu, or downgrade back to the Starter plan without losing your existing datasets.
